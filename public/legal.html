Clear rules for real work.

KREVUNO is designed to help people and businesses discover and confirm real short-term work while protecting privacy, payment transparency, safety and fair participation.

Important: No website can eliminate every legal, safety, employment, tax, payment or fraud risk in every country. Mandatory local law always controls. KREVUNO applies these baseline rules and may add jurisdiction-specific requirements where needed.

Worker fee

0% KREVUNO marketplace commission from the agreed worker pay at launch. Third-party banking, payout, tax or legally required charges may still apply.

Employer service fee

7% of agreed pay when a job becomes mutually confirmed, where KREVUNO payment processing is enabled. Any minimum, taxes or processor costs must be shown before confirmation.

Money flow

KREVUNO is designed so worker compensation is not treated as KREVUNO revenue. Where supported, regulated payment infrastructure routes worker funds to the worker/connected account and KREVUNO receives only its disclosed platform fee.

Mutual commitment

A worker's first acceptance records interest. The commitment begins only when the requester/employer also confirms the match. After mutual confirmation, both sides are expected to show up and perform in good faith.

Terms of Use

KREVUNO is a technology marketplace that helps users discover, post and coordinate work opportunities. KREVUNO does not guarantee that any job, worker, employer, payment, income level, outcome or relationship will be available or successful.

Users must be at least 18 years old at launch, provide truthful information, comply with applicable law, and only offer or accept work they are legally permitted and qualified to perform. Work authorization, immigration status, licensing, insurance, tax, wage, worker-classification and employment obligations remain subject to the law of the place where the work is performed.

No term is intended to waive rights that cannot legally be waived. KREVUNO may refuse, restrict or remove unlawful, unsafe, fraudulent, discriminatory, deceptive or abusive activity, subject to applicable notice, review and appeal rights.

Payments & Fees

Launch policy: KREVUNO does not deduct a marketplace commission from the worker's agreed pay. The employer/requester service fee is 7% of agreed pay where payment processing is enabled. The exact fee and any required taxes or processor costs must be disclosed before the employer confirms.

A worker's one-sided acceptance does not by itself charge an employer. The service fee becomes due only after both sides confirm the engagement. This protects both parties from unauthorized or accidental commitments.

When payment services are enabled, they may be provided by regulated third-party payment processors. Payment availability, settlement timing, refunds, chargebacks, taxes and payout eligibility can vary by country, payment method and provider rules.

Acceptance, Cancellation & No-Show Rules

Mutual confirmation creates a marketplace commitment. It does not give KREVUNO the power to physically compel anyone to work. Safety, health, emergencies and non-waivable legal rights always take priority.

If a confirmed participant cannot attend, KREVUNO records one of five standardized reasons:

1. Medical / emergency
2. Safety concern
3. Terms changed
4. Transport / weather
5. Other + explanation

A no-show report does not automatically punish the accused user. Reports are recorded for review. Only confirmed no-shows count toward account restrictions. Five confirmed no-shows within a rolling 180-day period trigger a temporary 14-day acceptance/posting suspension, subject to review, appeal and local-law requirements.

Ratings & Reputation

Both sides may rate each other from 1 to 10 only after a verified completed engagement. KREVUNO stores both the cumulative score and the number of ratings, so a profile can show information such as 210 points across 27 rated jobs together with the average.

Ratings must reflect genuine experience. KREVUNO does not sell positive ratings, require positive sentiment, or intentionally suppress legitimate negative feedback. Fraudulent, retaliatory, abusive or manipulated reviews may be investigated and removed under a documented moderation process.

Privacy & Location

KREVUNO collects only data reasonably needed for account security, matching, transactions, trust and legal compliance. Location sharing is optional unless a specific service genuinely requires it. Public map coordinates are deliberately reduced in precision; exact personal or home locations should not be exposed by the public nearby feed.

Users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or export personal data depending on applicable law. Sensitive data should not be inferred or used for ranking unless legally permitted and genuinely necessary. KREVUNO should not monitor a person's private life outside marketplace activity.

Safety, Equality & Prohibited Activity
  • No illegal goods, illegal services, trafficking, exploitation, violence, fraud or deceptive job postings.
  • No discrimination in hiring or access to work where prohibited by applicable law.
  • Licensed, regulated or hazardous work may require proof of qualification, permits, insurance or other safeguards.
  • Users must not request off-platform conduct intended to evade law, safety controls or legally required payments.
  • KREVUNO may use fraud, abuse and identity safeguards proportionate to risk, with human review for significant account restrictions where required.
Employment Status, Taxes & Local Law

Calling a participant an independent contractor does not determine legal status. Worker classification depends on the real facts and the law of each jurisdiction. Employers/requesters remain responsible for wage, hour, payroll, tax, insurance, workplace-safety, licensing and employment obligations that apply to them.

Travelers and visitors must have lawful authorization to perform paid work in the country or region where the work occurs. KREVUNO does not provide immigration status or work authorization.

Disputes, Account Actions & Liability

KREVUNO may investigate fraud, safety incidents, payment disputes, fake listings, repeated confirmed no-shows and manipulation of ratings. Significant restrictions should include an explanation and a reasonable review or appeal path where required by law.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, KREVUNO is not responsible for acts or omissions of independent users, third-party payment providers, transportation providers or external services. Nothing here excludes liability or consumer/worker rights that cannot legally be excluded.
